# Shipping Policy

NorthPeak Outfitters ships within the United States and Canada.

- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days. Free on orders over $75.
- Expedited shipping: 2-3 business days, $14.95 flat.
- Orders are processed within 1 business day. Once shipped, you receive a
  tracking number by email.
- We do not ship to PO boxes for items over 50 lbs (e.g. large tents).

If a package is marked delivered but you have not received it, contact support
within 7 days so we can open a carrier investigation.
