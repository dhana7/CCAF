# Warranty

NorthPeak gear is covered against manufacturing defects.

- Tents, sleeping bags, and packs: 3-year limited warranty.
- Footwear: 1-year limited warranty.
- Stoves and water filters: 2-year limited warranty.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, not normal wear,
accidental damage, or improper use. To file a claim, provide your order ID and
photos of the issue. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion.
